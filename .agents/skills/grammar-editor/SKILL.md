---
name: grammar-editor
description: Proofread Unreasonable Doubt drafts for spelling, grammar, punctuation, and consistency while preserving the author's claims and voice.
---

Proofread the requested text. Preserve meaning, equations, quotations, links, and the author's voice. Make direct corrections when intent is clear. Flag wording whose intended meaning is uncertain, and report substantive ambiguities separately from mechanical edits. Follow any spelling convention the user specifies; otherwise keep the convention already used in the draft consistent.

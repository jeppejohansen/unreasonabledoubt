---
name: structure-editor
description: Review or revise a blog draft paragraph by paragraph so each paragraph has one identifiable claim and enough support. Use for argument flow and paragraph structure, not proofreading alone.
---

Use Thomas Basbøll's key-sentence idea as a diagnostic: each paragraph should make one claim and then support, elaborate, or defend it. See [his description](https://inframethodology.cbs.dk/?p=5670). Treat his length and timing ideals as prompts for judgment, not fixed limits for a public blog.

First map the current paragraph sequence into one-sentence claims. Identify a paragraph that changes claims midway, repeats another paragraph, lacks support, or leaves a transition implicit. Check whether each section advances the opening puzzle toward the conclusion. Then revise the requested text or propose a reordered paragraph outline, preserving the author's position and marking gaps that require the author's input or a source.

When reporting, give the revised text or a concise paragraph map and the few structural decisions that matter most. Leave spelling and copy edits to a separate pass unless they are needed for clarity.

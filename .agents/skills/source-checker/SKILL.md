---
name: source-checker
description: Audit factual claims, citations, links, and outside assets in an Unreasonable Doubt draft. Use when the user asks to verify evidence or prepare a sourced post, not for internal model derivations alone.
---

Separate empirical claims, model assumptions, simulated results, and the author's interpretations. For each empirical claim in scope, inspect the cited source and confirm it supports the actual wording, population, date, and magnitude. Search for an authoritative primary source when a claim lacks one. Record uncertainty or a narrower defensible wording rather than manufacturing support.

Check that links resolve and that external material in `assets/` has enough source and attribution information for the draft. Keep source notes close to the relevant claim. Report only material issues in a compact list with the claim, evidence found, and the needed edit; make the requested edits when the evidence is clear.

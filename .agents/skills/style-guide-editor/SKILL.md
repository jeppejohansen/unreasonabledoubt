description: Edit an Unreasonable Doubt blog draft for the author's established voice, clarity, and model-led reasoning. Use for stylistic revision of prose, not a grammar-only pass or a new outline interview.
---

Read [the style guide](references/style-guide.md) before editing. For a question about the published voice, consult the extracted posts in `references/published_posts/`; ignore PDF extraction and web-page artifacts. Keep the writer's claim, degree of certainty, and examples intact unless the user asks for a substantive rewrite.

Edit toward a concrete opening puzzle, clearly introduced assumptions, a mechanism the reader can follow, a worked example, and a conditional conclusion. Use signposts only where they clarify a change in reasoning. Distinguish what the model shows from what evidence shows. When figures are discussed, make their reading order and causal step clear.

Return the edited passage or edit the requested file, and briefly identify any substantive questions the style pass exposed. Do not invent evidence or silently resolve an unclear argument.

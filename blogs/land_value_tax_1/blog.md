# One way to implement a land value tax.

Idea: combine two systems:
- deferred acceptance to access to lots
- use a second price sealed bid auction. (or rather an automated ascending auction due to obviously strategy proof mechanism design)
- Every developer, i.e., firms that buy land to develop on it (implying individuals building for themselves is excluded in this stage) needs to register in official auction system.
- Every city area (or outside cities even) is divided into smaller areas, where the land is somewhat assumed to be of homogenous quality. The firms needs to prememptively bid on these areas with an associated square meter price. Next they rank all their bids by preference (deferred acceptance).
- Concretely, once every third month, an simultanous auction of the land is held. Private individuals, can also bid on specific parcels. Each firm also submits a budget, so it can never exceed that.
- This way you will get efficient allocation of land resources. But also, you will get exact LVT of the entire country.
- Finally, it's very easy to implement.
